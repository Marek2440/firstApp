# International (non-Polish) market for custom brick models on demand (as of Oct 2026)

> Method note: direct page fetching (WebFetch/curl) was blocked by the session's egress proxy for all target sites (Etsy, brickmodeldesign.com, henryshomemodels.com, etc.). All facts below come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Etsy shop stats (sales and review counts) could not be read live. Many prices come from press articles from 2018 to 2020 and are flagged as **[PRE-2024]**. Where the search tool gave a page age, it is noted.

## 1. House/home replicas (B2C and realtor/developer gifts)

### Takeaway
There are three price tiers. (1) Premium assembled replicas by individual builders cost about $1,000 to $9,000+ and take 8 weeks to 8 months, usually priced per sq ft or by house type. (2) Etsy "facade kits" in genuine LEGO with printed instructions cost about $200 to $220, with roughly a 2-week design turnaround. (3) PDF-only digital designs cost about $50 to $150 on Etsy, or $30 to $80 on Fiverr. Nearly every seller is a one-person shop in the US. I found no dedicated house-replica brand in the UK, Germany, the Netherlands or Scandinavia, and only one in Australia (Micsar).

### Inferences
- The market is fragmented into micro-sellers. The best-known US brand (Little Brick Lane) had only 13 Etsy sales despite years of national press, which suggests demand at $1,500+ is thin. The productized ~$200 facade kit (Brick Keepsake, the realtor-gift listings) looks like the format that scales.
- Price anchors for a business model: digital-only $30 to $150, kit $200 to $300, assembled $400 to $3,000 (facade) and $3,000 to $9,000+ (full house with interior).
- In Western/Northern Europe there appears to be a whitespace for a productized "your house as a kit" offer. Existing EU and UK players are B2B commission studios.

### Gaps
- Live Etsy sales and review counts for Brick Keepsake, the $50 PDF seller and the kit sellers (Etsy was not fetchable).
- Brick Model Design lead times and price examples.
- Current (2025 to 2026) Little Brick Lane status and pricing.
- No UK, DE, NL or Scandinavian consumer house-replica brand was found. This may be a search limitation (local-language Etsy shops).

## 2. Professional builders (LCPs, Bright Bricks, commission studios) and price per piece

### Takeaway
There are only about 21 to 24 LEGO Certified Professionals worldwide. None publishes rates, and commissions are quoted per project. Independent studios publish floors of about $2,500 (large sculptures) to $5,000 (minimum commission), and corporate bespoke projects start around $10,000. I found no reliable published price per stud or per piece.

### Cited Findings
- About 24 LCPs globally, per the Wikipedia article on Ryan McNaught (updated late 2025). He is the only LCP in the Southern Hemisphere. — [Wikipedia: Ryan McNaught](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_McNaught)
- Brick Fanatics reported 21 LCPs (UK, Italy, Denmark, Canada, US, Japan, Singapore; article undated). LEGO Ambassador Network said 23 in April 2022 **[PRE-2024]**. — [Brick Fanatics](https://www.brickfanatics.com/lego-is-hiring-two-new-certified-professionals); [LAN](https://lan.lego.com/news/overview/new-lego-certified-professionals-joining-the-lego-certified-professional-program-r307/)
- LEGO's FAQ says LCPs charge for their work and that restrictions apply. — [LEGO.com LCP](https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus/lego-certified-professionals)
- Brick Architect (updated Apr 2022) says LCPs are no longer allowed to create custom sets (kits), which conflicts with the LEGO FAQ's mention of custom models. — [Brick Architect unofficial sets](https://brickarchitect.com/unofficial_sets/)
- LCPs can buy bulk bricks from LEGO for an annual enrollment fee. — [BrickWiki](http://www.brickwiki.info/wiki/lego_certified_professional/)
- **Bright Bricks (UK, Bordon):**
  - No published commission prices.
  - Builds have included a Rolls-Royce jet engine, a 35 ft Christmas tree and Tower Bridge. The largest, a Tower Bridge for Land Rover, took 24 builders 3 months.
  - Uses CAD (Solid Edge).
  - Also sells retail sets, e.g. Ashford Castle at €150 for 1,411 pcs.
  - — [Bright Bricks builds](https://bright-bricks.com/builds/); [DEVELOP3D profile](https://develop3d.com/profiles/brick-by-brick-bright-bricks-lego-cad-solid-edge-st10-design/)
- Brickerbuilds' large-scale sculptures start at $2,500+ and are quoted by scale and structural complexity. — [Brickerbuilds](https://brickerbuilds.com/pages/large-scale-custom-brick-installations/)
- One commission studio sets a minimum commission of $5,000 USD. Another says simple models cost a few hundred dollars and large ones reach tens of thousands. — [buildalifeinpieces](https://www.buildalifeinpieces.com/commissions); [Belle Ve Bricks](https://bellevebricks.com/custom-lego-commission-work/)
- 3D Supercar Bricks sells a commissioned custom brick supercar for about $490 as a complete kit. — [3dsupercarbricks](https://3dsupercarbricks.com/products/commission-your-custom-lego%C2%AE-supercar)
- ATL Brick publishes a commissioning FAQ. — [ATL Brick](https://atlbrick.com/blogs/faqs/everything-you-want-to-know-about-commissioning-a-build-out-of-lego%C2%AE-bricks)
- Brick Architect (Tom Alphin) offers commissions ("Tell your story using LEGO"). — [Brick Architect commissions](https://brickarchitect.com/commissions/)

### Inferences
- With about 24 LCPs and a handful of UK and US studios, the high end is a services business (event, brand and attraction installations). Developer and architectural models are a sub-segment priced in the thousands, not a mass market.

### Gaps
- No credible published per-stud or per-piece commission rate was found. Rates such as "$0.10 to $0.50 per piece" circulate in forums but were not verified, so they are not reported here.
- Bright Bricks' revenue and headcount, and real-estate developer case studies with prices.

## 3. B2B branded/promotional brick sets (corporate, real estate, developers)

### Takeaway
There are two tiers. Chinese OEMs using compatible bricks offer about $10 to $12 per set at an MOQ of 200, plus about $300 per design sample. Western genuine-LEGO kitters charge $5 to $150+ per set at an MOQ of about 100 (DD Bricks) or a $3,500 minimum project (BuildaMOC), with 4 to 8 week lead times. Real-estate "your development as a kit" is an explicit, marketed vertical.

### Cited Findings
- **Corporate MOCs (corporatemocs.com, China):**
  - Trade-show sets from $10 per set, MOQ 200. — [category page](https://corporatemocs.com/category/trade-show-corporate-lego-set-gifts/)
  - Real-estate branded house sets: "$10~12/set in hand" including outer packaging and printed instructions, MOQ 200. Page age about 1 to 1.5 years. — [real-estate house set](https://corporatemocs.com/branded-house-lego-set-for-real-estate-promotional-gifts/)
  - Christmas sets: MOQ 200, $300 per unique design for a physical sample, $12 to $42 per set for 10 to 30 cm sets. — [Christmas page](https://corporatemocs.com/christmas-lego-gifts-ideas-for-new-year-corporate-giveaways/)
  - Berkeley CPU set page lists MOQ 200 but a sample order of 500. The contact email on that page is misspelled (sales@corproatemocs.com), a weak quality signal. — [CPU page](https://corporatemocs.com/berkeley-lego-cpu-bricks-set-gifts-for-trade-shows/)
- **DD Bricks (ddbricks.com; production in Germany, USA, Thailand; genuine LEGO):**
  - Current corporate page: MOQ 100 sets. Mini (XS) from $5 to $15, Small $16 to $35, Medium $36 to $70, XL $150+.
  - Older pages say MOQ 50, or "no minimum".
  - Bespoke corporate projects "typically start at 10,000 USD".
  - Lead time 6 to 8 weeks (elsewhere 4 to 8).
  - Lists real estate among the industries it serves.
  - — [Corporate gifts](https://ddbricks.com/corporate-gifts/); [Home](https://ddbricks.com/); [Esports page](https://ddbricks.com/corporate-gift-ideas/lego-gifts-esports-gaming-companies/); [Engineering](https://ddbricks.com/industries/engineering-manufacturing/)
  - DD Bricks also claims 80,000+ BrickLink/BrickOwl orders tested, a signal that it runs a large parts-sourcing operation. — [DD Bricks blog](https://ddbricks.com/post/bricklink-vs-brickowl-unveiling-the-best-lego-marketplace-for-you/)
- **BuildaMOC (buildamoc.com; genuine LEGO parts):**
  - "Turn your product, building or brand into a custom brick kit."
  - Business minimum: $3,500 total project value, whether one large display or thousands of kits.
  - The price includes UV-printed pieces, booklets and custom packaging.
  - Quote inputs: idea, quantity, date and target cost per kit.
  - Has a dedicated page "Custom Brick Kits for Real Estate and Construction Companies".
  - Also sells retail MOC kits (e.g. Modular Library, BrickVault).
  - — [For business](https://buildamoc.com/pages/for-business); [Real estate & construction](https://buildamoc.com/pages/kits-of-buildings); [Small kits/giveaways](https://buildamoc.com/pages/small-kits-giveaways)
- Other players: Corporate Brick (corporatebrick.com, custom corporate LEGO gifts for branding) and Playwell Bricks, which publishes a pricing page. — [Corporate Brick](https://corporatebrick.com/); [Playwell pricing](https://playwell-bricks.com/pages/pricing)
- **Realtor closing-gift context:**
  - Common benchmark: 1% to 5% of gross commission. Typical spend is $50 to $200 (up to $300); vendors cite $100 to $500. — [iCustomLabel 2026](https://icustomlabel.com/blogs/real-estate-marketing-and-gifts/how-much-do-realtors-spend-on-closing-gifts); [ClosingDayGifts 2026](https://closingdaygifts.com/blogs/news/how-much-should-a-real-estate-agent-spend-on-a-closing-gift-2026-guide)
  - BizGift poll: 66.67% spend about $100. The sample is tiny (about 9 responses) and the page is about 3 years old **[PRE-2024]**. — [BizGift](https://bizgift.com/blog/business-gifts/realtors-top-3-answers-from-our-recent-polls)
  - US IRS business gift deduction cap is $25 per recipient per year (IRC §274(b)), a vendor's reading. — [Pacific Gift Box](https://www.pacificgiftbox.com/blog/do-realtors-give-closing-gifts-etiquette-timing-budget)

### Inferences
- Margins: OEM landed cost is about $10 to $12 per set (compatible bricks) against Western genuine-LEGO small sets at $16 to $35 and B2C facade kits at about $205. This implies large gross margins on design and personalization rather than on bricks. Design and sample fees (about $300) recover the one-off design cost.
- A $205 custom facade kit sits at or above the typical realtor closing-gift budget ($50 to $200). A developer-level promo (one design × hundreds of buyers) fits the OEM MOQ-200 model much better than one-off house kits.

### Gaps
- Actual margins or revenue for Corporate MOCs, DD Bricks or BuildaMOC (none disclosed).
- No sizing of the realtor closing-gift market (total US $) was found. Only per-agent spend heuristics exist.

## 4. Platforms for custom MOC instructions (Rebrickable, BrickLink Designer Program, Brickit)

### Takeaway
Digital instructions are a low-ticket market. The average Rebrickable Premium MOC sells for about $9, with commission tiers of 8% to 20%. The BrickLink Designer Program is a crowdfunded, curated pipeline paying a 5% royalty. Brickit is a scanning app (about 400k MAU, seed-funded), not a custom-design service.

### Cited Findings
- **Rebrickable Premium MOCs:**
  - Commission since May 2025:
    - Designer Plan: 8%, 13% or 18%.
    - Free/Pro: 10%, 15% or 20%.
    - Price bands: $1 to $2.99, $3 to $19.99, $20+.
  - Payment processor fees are about $0.30 + 2.9 to 3.6%. With PayPal the designer is paid immediately and invoiced monthly. With Stripe the commission is deducted.
  - — [Rebrickable fees](https://rebrickable.com/help/mocs-fees/); [Selling Premium MOCs](https://rebrickable.com/help/selling-premium-mocs/)
  - Rebrickable says the average purchase price from actual sales is about USD 9, with far more selling below that than above. — [Rebrickable tips](https://rebrickable.com/help/tips-for-popular-mocs/)
  - Third-party guidance: $3 to $5 small, $5 to $10 medium, $10 to $15+ large. — [Earl of Bricks](https://theearlofbricks.com/blog-studio-publish-moc/); [Brickset forum](https://forum.brickset.com/discussion/27724/pricing-for-a-premium-rebrickable-moc)
  - MOCPlans merged into Rebrickable (consolidation). — [Rebrickable blog](https://rebrickable.com/blog/154/mocplans-has-merged-with-rebrickable/)
- **BrickLink Designer Program (now "Designer Program"):**
  - Fan designs are built in BrickLink Studio, voted on, and 5 finalists per round go to crowdfunding.
  - The designer earns 5% of net sales. Repeat finalists reportedly reach seven figures.
  - — [Wikipedia BDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BrickLink_Designer_Program); [HothBricks](https://en.hothbricks.com/lego-ideas-et-bricklink-designer-program-combien-gagnent-les-fan-designers/)
  - Older rule: up to 30,000 copies per set and 3,000 pre-orders needed (illustrative 3,000 × $199.99 × 5% ≈ $30k). Hypothetical, **[PRE-2024]**. — [ENBRIC](https://enbric.com/blogs/income-of-about-30-000-usd-bricklink-designer-program-season-6-has-started)
  - Series 5 closed 24 June 2025 with all 5 funded but none sold out. Series 8 fully funded. Series 9 has been announced. — [Brick Fanatics June 2025](https://www.brickfanatics.com/lego-bricklink-designer-program-june-2025); [Series 8](https://www.brickfanatics.com/lego-bricklink-designer-program-series-8-sets-fully-funded); [Series 9](https://www.brickfanatics.com/bricklink-designer-program-series-9-prices-pre-orders)
- **BrickLink marketplace:**
  - About 139,800 MOC items, 97k parts and 21.9k sets in the catalog (page about 1.4 years old). — [BrickLink](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/main.page)
  - BrickLink (owned by LEGO) closed its marketplace in 35 countries (India, Brazil, Turkey, Egypt, Costa Rica…) effective 31 Jan 2026. About 82,791 members were affected and 646 sellers suspended. This is relevant to parts sourcing for kit makers. — [Brick Fanatics](https://www.brickfanatics.com/lego-bricklink-marketplace-35-countries); [New Elementary, 3 Dec 2025](https://www.newelementary.com/2025/12/bricklink-closures-official-statement.html); [Wikipedia BrickLink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BrickLink)
- **Brickit:**
  - AI app that scans a pile of bricks and suggests builds.
  - Seed round led by Sound Media Ventures (Oct 2022), amount undisclosed. Investors also include RTP Global and Joint Journey.
  - About 400k+ MAU, mainly US, UK and DE (JJ Capital case study).
  - HQ is listed inconsistently: Wilmington, Moscow or Tbilisi.
  - Fan-made, not LEGO-affiliated.
  - — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/brickit_rebuild_your_lego); [JJ Capital](https://jj.capital/portfolio-startup-case-brickit); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/brickit); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brickit-app/id1477221636)

### Inferences
- Generic MOC instructions earn about $9 per sale. A personalized house design earns $30 to $150 per order on Fiverr or Etsy. Personalization supports roughly a 5 to 15× premium over off-the-shelf instructions.

### Gaps
- Rebrickable aggregate GMV and top-designer earnings. BrickLink Studio usage numbers.

## 5. Startups automating "house → kit" and market size data

### Inferences
- The gap in automation is notable. The pieces exist (Stud.io, Rebrickable/BrickLink parts APIs, AI image models), but no one has productized photo → kit with venture funding. The human design step (about 2 weeks on Etsy, 2 to 13 days on Fiverr) remains the bottleneck and the cost driver.
- With LEGO revenue around DKK 83.5bn and an adult share of about 20% to 25%, the adult segment is roughly DKK 17 to 21bn (about $2.5 to 3bn). This is a rough estimate built on third-party share figures.

### Gaps
- No credible market-size estimate for the custom/MOC economy, alt-brick OEM kits, or the custom house-replica niche.
- No total US realtor closing-gift market size was found.
- No documented failed startups in the house-to-kit space were found. This does not prove none existed.
- The official LEGO adult revenue share was not confirmed from the annual report text.
